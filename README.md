# COVID-19, Economic Conditions, and U.S. Housing Prices

*An exploratory analysis of the relationship between the COVID-19 pandemic, state-level economic indicators, and housing prices across the United States.*

## Overview

The COVID-19 pandemic produced substantial changes in economic conditions and housing markets across the United States. This project examines how changes in pandemic conditions and state-level economic indicators were associated with changes in housing prices.

The analysis combines data on COVID-19 cases and deaths, housing prices, unemployment, and other state-level economic indicators to explore geographic and temporal patterns across the United States.

## Research Questions

This project investigates questions including:

- How did housing prices change across U.S. states during and after the COVID-19 pandemic?
- How were housing prices associated with state-level unemployment and other economic indicators?
- Did states experiencing different levels of COVID-19 impact exhibit different housing-market trends?
- Which economic factors were most strongly associated with changes in housing prices?

## Data

The analysis combines multiple publicly available datasets, including:

- **COVID-19 data** – State-level COVID-19 cases and deaths
- **Zillow Home Value Index (ZHVI)** – Measures of typical home values and housing-market changes across U.S. states
- **State unemployment data** – State-level unemployment rates from the Bureau of Labor Statistics
- **Additional economic indicators** – State-level measures used to examine broader economic conditions

## Methods

The project follows a data-analysis workflow consisting of:

- Data collection and integration
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Exploratory data analysis
- Geographic and temporal visualization
- Correlation analysis

- Comparison of economic conditions across states
